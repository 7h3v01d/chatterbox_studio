Sample Voice Script:

"Wait—did you actually hear that? Yesterday at 4:15 PM, three brisk winds 

swept through the quiet valley, carrying the faint, crisp scent of pine and 

rain. While sixty-seven curious onlookers gathered near the old lighthouse, 

Dr. Vance calmly checked his silver stopwatch and whispered, 'Time doesn't 

wait for hesitation, does it?' Suddenly, a bright flare shot into the twilight sky, 

sparking a chorus of gasps, laughter, and astonished applause across the 

entire harbor."


Notes: 
======

Why this paragraph works well for testing:

• Emotional and tonal shifts. It moves from a surprised question to calm narration, drops into a quiet whisper, and finishes with high-energy excitement. This checks how well the model adapts its delivery rather than sounding flat.
• Pacing and punctuation. The em-dash, commas, ellipses-style pauses, and quotation marks test whether the voice takes natural breaths and changes inflection around dialogue.
• Phonetic variety. Words like three brisk winds, crisp, sixty-seven, and twilight force the model to handle tricky consonant clusters, sibilants ("s" and "sh" sounds), and varied vowel lengths.
• Numbers and abbreviations. Including a time (4:15 PM), a hyphenated number (sixty-seven), and a title (Dr.) ensures the text-to-speech engine normalizes text properly without stumbling.
